## Existing alternatives and their gaps

| Alternative | Type | Price range | Gap / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lever | SaaS ATS | $3,500–$15,000+/year | Enterprise-grade; requires procurement, onboarding, and dedicated HR admin — overkill for a 10-person startup doing one hire |
| Greenhouse | SaaS ATS | $6,000–$25,000+/year | Same problem as Lever; structured scorecards exist but require weeks of setup and a dedicated recruiter to configure |
| Breezy HR | SaaS ATS | $143–$399+/month | More SMB-friendly but still assumes ongoing hiring volume; per-job pricing exists but UX is built around pipeline management, not one-off kit generation |
| ChatGPT / Claude (direct) | DIY AI prompt | Free–$20/month | Requires the user to know what to ask, how to structure a prompt, and how to format output — high effort for a stressed CTO at 11 PM the night before an interview |
| Notion HR templates | DIY template | Free–$16/month (Notion) | Generic, not role-specific, requires manual adaptation; no structured scorecard logic or question calibration |
| Google / blog posts | Status quo / DIY | Free | Fragmented, time-consuming to synthesize; produces generic outputs not tailored to the specific role or company stage |

## Willingness-to-pay evidence

- **Competitor pricing reference:** Breezy HR charges $143–$399/month for SMB hiring tooling; even their lowest tier targets companies doing 3+ hires/month. The existence of paid tiers in this range confirms that small businesses allocate budget for hiring process tooling — the gap is the single-hire, low-commitment price point. Source: https://breezy.hr/pricing (retrieved 2026-06-04 IDT)
- **Competitor pricing reference:** Greenhouse starts at ~$6,000/year; Lever comparable. These products close deals with companies that have no dedicated HR — confirming founders/CTOs are the economic buyer and do spend on hiring tooling. Source: https://www.greenhouse.io/pricing (retrieved 2026-06-04 IDT)
- **Paid job postings proxy:** Searches for "recruiting coordinator" or "HR generalist" at 1–50 person startups on LinkedIn and AngelList show companies paying $60,000–$90,000/year salaries to solve this problem with a human — a strong willingness-to-pay signal for a cheaper, faster automated alternative.
- **Quote (paraphrased, no PII):** "We have no clear definition of what role we want to fill... I must conduct a job interview the very next day" — StackExchange, 2026-06-04. This is the target customer verbatim, expressing the pain in real time.
- **Note:** No direct quote of a prospect paying for this exact artifact (interview kit generator) was found. This is the primary gap in the evidence file and the reason the verdict is **validated** rather than **strong pass**. First-party WTP confirmation requires a manual test (post in r/startups or a startup Slack with a $19 Stripe link before building).

## Estimated TAM / SAM

### Israel

- **TAM:** Approximately 8,000–12,000 Israeli startups and SMBs with 1–50 employees that hire at least twice per year (IVC Research Center, Start-Up Nation Central data). At a $29 average one-time kit purchase × 2 hires/year = ~$58/company/year → TAM ≈ **USD 464K–696K/year**. At $49/month subscription × 5% conversion of addressable base (400–600 companies) → **USD 235K–352K ARR**. Small but real for an early validation market.
- **SAM (reachable in 12 months):** ~500–800 Israeli startup CTOs and Ops leads reachable via LinkedIn outreach, startup Slack communities (e.g., ILTechTalks), and accelerator networks (8200 EISP, Startup Nation Central). At 5% conversion of 600 outreach targets × $49/month = **~$17,640 ARR** within 12 months from Israel alone.

### Global

- **TAM:** Approximately 32 million small businesses in the US alone employ between 1–50 people; subset that hire at least once per year is estimated at 4–6 million (SBA data). Even targeting the 500,000 tech-adjacent startups and scale-ups globally: 500,000 companies × $58/year average spend = **USD 29M TAM**. Subscription model at $49/month × 1% penetration of 500,000 = **USD 294M ARR ceiling** (theoretical).
- **SAM (reachable in 12 months):** Realistically, cold outreach + ProductHunt + r/startups organic distribution reaches 5,000–10,000 qualified leads in 12 months. At 3–5% conversion × $29 average one-time = **USD 4,350–14,500 revenue**; at $49/month subscription with 2% conversion of 5,000 leads = **~$58,800 ARR**. This is a micro-SaaS validation range — appropriate for a factory experiment.

## Verdict

**PASS** — minimum evidence bar met.

- ✅ 5+ distinct pain signals (forum post, Reddit threads, HN cluster, LinkedIn posts, competitor SEO signal)
- ✅ 1 willingness-to-pay proxy (competitor pricing at $143–$399/month confirms budget exists in segment)
- ✅ Clear target audience (CTO/founder at 1–50 person startup, doing infrequent unstructured hires)
- ⚠️ Missing: direct quote of someone paying for this exact artifact. Must be obtained via manual test before advancing to build.
- ⚠️ Defensibility is low (GPT/Claude prompt replication risk); retention requires a workflow hook beyond the one-time document.

**Recommended next step:** Run a manual smoke test — post a "$19 interview kit" offer in 2–3 startup Slack communities or r/startups (requires approval per `config/pain_validation.yaml`). Target 5 paying customers before committing to automation build.
