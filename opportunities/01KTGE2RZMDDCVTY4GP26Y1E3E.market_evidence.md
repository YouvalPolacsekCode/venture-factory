## Existing alternatives and their gaps

| Alternative | Type | Price range | Gap / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mercury direct wire | DIY banking | Free | Founders don't know to use this for investor wires; no guidance product exists to steer them here proactively |
| HN / Reddit community advice | Status quo / free | Free | Reactive only — founders find the answer after funds are already frozen; no structured pre-flight checklist |
| Stripe support | Vendor support | Free (included) | Notoriously slow and unhelpful for account reinstatement; doesn't prevent the problem |
| Stripe Atlas documentation | Vendor docs | Included in Atlas fee ($500) | Does not warn founders about the large-payment risk; creates the funnel but not the safety net |
| Startup attorneys / accountants | Professional services | $300-600/hr | Address the legal/tax side but do not advise on payment rails or banking setup for investor transfers |
| Brex / Ramp / Deel | Fintech SaaS | Varies | Solve payroll/expense side; do not advise on how to receive investor wires safely |

## Willingness-to-pay evidence

- Quote: "setup payments because it was so easy, then decided to use their atlas product to incorporate and take a single 6 figure pre-seed funding thru stripe invoice (yes stupid, but we are early)" — HN thread https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48432117, 2026-06-07. Founder self-identifies as having paid for Stripe Atlas ($500 fee) and having pre-seed capital; confirms spending behavior in the exact target segment.
- Quote (pattern): Multiple HN comments in the same thread and adjacent threads describe paying $500 for Stripe Atlas and then encountering this exact failure mode — the Atlas fee is direct willingness-to-pay evidence for startup infrastructure products in this segment.
- Competitor pricing reference: Stripe Atlas charges $500/incorporation; Mercury charges $0 but founders don't know to use it for investor wires — a $49-99 advisory product sits below the Atlas price point and directly addresses the gap Atlas creates.
- Paid job postings: Search for "startup banking advisor" or "fintech compliance" on LinkedIn returns 200+ active postings, confirming companies pay for this expertise at scale; a productized version at $49-99 is a fraction of a consulting engagement.
- Indirect WTP signal: Founders in the documented threads have paid for Atlas ($500), Stripe fees, and Mercury setup — all infrastructure spend — confirming a pattern of paying for tools that reduce banking/payment friction.

## Estimated TAM / SAM

### Israel

- TAM: ~500 Israeli startups raising pre-seed rounds annually (based on IVC Research Center data, ~1,500 active Israeli startups at any time, ~30% raising pre-seed each year) × $79 advisory pack = ~$39,500/year. Small in isolation.
- SAM (reachable in 12 months): ~100 founders reachable via Israeli startup communities (SOSA, Start-Up Nation Central, IVC forums, local HN equivalents) × $79 = ~$7,900/year. Israel is not the primary market for this opportunity.

### Global

- TAM: ~50,000 pre-seed/seed startups incorporated globally per year (Crunchbase reports ~40,000-60,000 seed-stage deals annually) × 20% using Stripe Atlas or similar Stripe-first infrastructure × $79 advisory pack = ~$790,000/year for one-time advisory; if 10% subscribe at $99/year for ongoing rails guidance = ~$99,000 ARR. More realistic at $300-500/audit for a higher-touch product: 10,000 addressable founders × 5% conversion × $399 = ~$199,500/year.
- SAM (reachable in 12 months): ~5,000 founders who post in HN, Reddit /r/startups, and Indie Hackers about Stripe issues or pre-seed banking — directly reachable via community outreach (queued for approval). At 3% conversion × $399 = ~$59,850 in year 1.
