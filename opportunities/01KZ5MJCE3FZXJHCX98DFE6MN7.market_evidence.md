## Existing alternatives and their gaps

| Alternative | Type | Price range | Gap / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bonsai | SaaS (contract + invoice) | $17–$52/month | Offers contract templates and deposits but buries the feature; workflow does not enforce deposit-before-kickoff as a hard gate |
| HoneyBook | SaaS (CRM + payment) | $19–$79/month | Designed for creative studios; onboarding is heavy; deposit feature exists but is optional and not front-and-center |
| FreshBooks | SaaS (accounting + invoice) | $17–$55/month | Strong invoicing and late-payment reminders; no upfront commitment enforcement; focused on post-work billing |
| Wave | Freemium SaaS | Free + 2.9% transaction fee | No deposit or contract tooling; pure invoicing play; free tier attracts low-commitment users |
| DocuSign + Stripe (DIY) | DIY combination | $15–$45/month combined | Requires technical setup; no unified freelancer-specific flow; most freelancers cannot configure it |
| Status quo (email + bank transfer) | DIY / ignore | $0 | No enforcement; ~29% of invoices paid late; this is the dominant 'solution' |

## Estimated TAM / SAM

### Israel

- TAM: Israel has approximately 350,000 registered freelancers (Central Bureau of Statistics, 2023). Targeting web dev, design, and creative services (est. 25% = 87,500 freelancers). At $180/year (conservative ACV for a deposit-enforcement tool below existing competitors): **87,500 × $180 = $15.75M**
- SAM (reachable in 12 months): Freelancers active on LinkedIn Israel + local Facebook freelancer groups + Israeli dev communities (est. 8,000 reachable with direct outreach): **8,000 × $180 = $1.44M**

### Global

- TAM: ~73 million freelancers globally (Statista 2023 estimate); targeting English-speaking markets in web dev, design, creative (est. 12M). At $180/year ACV: **12,000,000 × $180 = $2.16B** (realistic serviceable slice is far smaller)
- SAM (reachable in 12 months): Freelancers reachable via r/freelance, IndieHackers, Contra, Toptal public profiles, and dev.to (est. 150,000 reachable contacts): **150,000 × $180 = $27M** — capturing even 0.5% = $135K ARR in year one
