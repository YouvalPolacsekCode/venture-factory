## Existing alternatives and their gaps

| Alternative | Type | Price range | Gap / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| DocSend (Dropbox) | SaaS | $45–150/user/mo (billed annually) | Expensive for SMBs; no DOCX/XLSX native rendering; watermarking only on Business+ plan; NDA gating requires Business tier |
| Notion + share link | DIY | Free–$16/user/mo | Zero per-page analytics; no NDA gating; no download controls; no dynamic watermarking |
| Pitch | SaaS | Free–$25/user/mo | Presentation-only (no PDF/DOCX/XLSX); no NDA gating; limited analytics depth |
| Google Drive + link | DIY / status quo | Free | No view analytics, no download prevention, no watermarking, no NDA flow |
| PandaDoc | SaaS (e-sign focus) | $19–$49/user/mo | Oriented toward e-signature workflows; page-level analytics available but limited; data rooms are add-on; weaker engagement tracking |
| Paperflite | SaaS | $50–$85/user/mo | Strong analytics but enterprise pricing; limited data room branding; XLSX/video support inconsistent |
| DealRoom / Ansarada | SaaS (M&A focus) | $500–$3,000+/mo | Built for M&A due diligence; massively overpriced for sales/BD use; steep learning curve |
