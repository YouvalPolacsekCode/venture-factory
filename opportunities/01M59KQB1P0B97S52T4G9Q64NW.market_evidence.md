## Existing alternatives and their gaps

| Alternative | Type | Price range | Gap / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthchecks.io | SaaS | Free – $80/mo | Generic heartbeat monitor; not per-user-account aware; operators must instrument themselves | 
| Cronitor | SaaS | $19 – $79/mo | Job-level monitoring only; no concept of per-user data freshness or user-facing alerts |
| Datadog / New Relic | Enterprise SaaS | $15–$100+/mo | Over-engineered for solo SaaS operators; no user-facing notification layer |
| DIY (custom scripts + email) | DIY | $0 (time cost) | Common solution; low switching incentive to paid tool |
| PagerDuty | Enterprise SaaS | $21+/mo | Ops-team focused; no user-facing alert concept |

## Willingness-to-pay evidence

- Competitor pricing reference: Cronitor, $19–$79/month, https://cronitor.io/pricing — confirms segment pays for job monitoring, but at generic level
- Competitor pricing reference: Healthchecks.io, $0–$80/month, https://healthchecks.io/pricing/ — strong free-tier absorption reduces paid WTP for small operators
- Paid job postings: 0 found for "data freshness monitoring" or "per-user pipeline alerting" as a standalone role
- Direct quote: "two accounts on the same instance hadn't received new stats for 42 days before one user emailed us" — Analytodon maintainer, GitHub issue #131, 2026-10-19 (operational pain confirmed, but no payment action taken)
- **No evidence of anyone paying specifically for per-user data-freshness watchdogging as a standalone product.** Existing paid tools solve the adjacent problem (cron health) but not the specific user-facing notification layer.

## Estimated TAM / SAM

### Israel
- TAM: Approximately 200–400 solo/small-team SaaS operators in Israel running scheduled data-collection jobs × $300/year = ~$60K–$120K. Too small to be primary market.
- SAM (reachable in 12 months): ~50–100 operators reachable via LinkedIn + GitHub outreach = ~$15K–$30K.

### Global
- TAM: Estimated 15,000–40,000 solo/small-team SaaS products globally with per-user background data collection × $300/year = ~$4.5M–$12M. However, free-tier competitor absorption and DIY preference suggest addressable fraction is ≤10%.
- SAM (reachable in 12 months): ~500–1,000 operators via GitHub issue searches, Indie Hackers, and HN outreach = ~$150K–$300K realistic ceiling in year 1.

## Verdict

**INCONCLUSIVE.** The primary source is a single, concrete operational failure. Adjacent pain signals exist in HN/Reddit discussions, and generic cron-monitoring competitors confirm some segment-level WTP. However, the five-distinct-pain-quotes bar is not met from publicly available sources, no one is paying specifically for per-user data-freshness notification as a standalone product, and free-tier alternatives dominate the small-operator segment.
