## Existing alternatives and their gaps

| Alternative | Type | Price range | Gap / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Docsend | SaaS | $45–150/month | Secure sharing with analytics, but no templated data room structure or document checklists; assumes you already know what to include | 
| Visible.vc | SaaS | $79–199/month | Strong for investor reporting and updates, but overkill and expensive for a pre-revenue founder doing their first round | 
| Capshare | SaaS | $65/month | Primarily cap table management; data room is secondary feature, not templated for first-time founders | 
| Notion community templates | DIY / free | $0 | Exist but are generic, incomplete, and require founders to know what to populate; no guided prompts or document-level explainers | 
| Lawyers / advisors | Human advisory | $300–600/hour | Expensive, slow, overkill for early pre-revenue rounds; founders defer until they have to pay | 
| Status quo (Google Drive, ad hoc) | DIY | $0 | Most common 'solution'; founders build a folder on the fly when investor asks; causes delays, missed docs, and lost deals |
